About LeadShift

Since 1997.

LeadShift has helped more than 200 organizations build their leaders and leadership teams, and many of our clients have stayed more than a decade. Headquartered in Worcester, Massachusetts, we work with companies across the United States. Our clients are concentrated in financial services, insurance, life sciences and manufacturing.

Why we exist.

Every growing company eventually faces the same question: will the team that got you here get you there? We help CEOs build the managers, leaders and leadership teams the business needs, so it performs without them carrying it.
