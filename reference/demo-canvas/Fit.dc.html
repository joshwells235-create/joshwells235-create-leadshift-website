Before you call.

Is the problem my leadership team, or the leaders on it?

Look at where things break. If capable leaders run their own areas well but the team struggles to debate, commit and hold each other accountable, the gap is the team. If the team works well together but some leaders aren't ready for what the business needs next, the gap is the leaders. Often it's both, and the Growth Index shows which.

Who does LeadShift work with?

CEOs and leadership teams of growing companies. Since 1997, LeadShift has worked with more than 200 organizations, many of them for more than a decade. Clients are concentrated in financial services, insurance, life sciences and manufacturing.
