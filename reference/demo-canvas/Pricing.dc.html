Three things move a price inside its range.

How many leaders take part

Seats and managers are priced per person, so a cohort of twelve costs more than a cohort of six.

How much one-on-one coaching runs alongside

Group work sets the common language. Coaching hours are where each leader applies it to their own work.

How long the program runs

Most teams take six months for ALIGN. A longer program means more sessions and more follow-through.
