## The Growth Index [concept]

- Complimentary for CEOs. About twenty minutes for the CEO, plus a short behavioral assessment for each leader.
- The CEO chooses the five of twelve leadership priorities the strategy depends on and rates each leader privately. The ratings stay with the CEO; leaders never see them.
- A LeadShift partner walks the CEO through the report, leader by leader. It shows whether the gap is how the team works together, how the leaders are growing, or both.
- **Profile fit** compares how each leader naturally works with the leadership the five priorities call for. **Seat fit** compares it with what the current role needs. Both describe fit, not ability or potential.
- The twelve priorities and their keys: `c_drive_ownership` Drive and Ownership; `c_delegates_authority` Delegates Authority; `c_exec_discipline` Executional Discipline; `c_personal_adaptability` Personal Adaptability; `c_decision_making` Decision Making; `c_communicates` Communicates Effectively; `c_strategic_thinking` Strategic Focus; `c_self_awareness` Self-Awareness; `c_change_leadership` Change Leadership; `c_right_people_seats` Right People, Right Seats; `c_develops_people` Develops People; `c_works_across` Works Across the Business. One-line descriptions are in the concept.
- App entry: `https://leadshift-cci.vercel.app/start?priorities=<five keys, comma-separated>`.
- No retests have been done yet. [Josh 2026-10-20]
- Sample report images in `assets/images/` are for a fictional company. Caption: "Pages from a sample report for a fictional company. Yours is built from your strategy, your ratings and your leaders' assessments."
