The Growth Index

A complimentary assessment for CEOs.

The CEO chooses the five of twelve leadership priorities their strategy depends on and rates each leader privately, and each leader completes a short behavioral assessment. It shows whether the gap is how the team works together, how the leaders are growing, or both. A LeadShift partner walks the CEO through what it shows.
