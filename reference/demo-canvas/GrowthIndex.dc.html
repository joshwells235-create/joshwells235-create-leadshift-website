How long does the Growth Index take?

About twenty minutes for you, plus a short assessment for each of your leaders.

Will my leaders see my ratings?

No. Your ratings stay with you, and your leaders never see them.

What do I get from the Growth Index, and when?

Once your leaders finish their short assessments, a LeadShift partner walks you through your report, leader by leader. Then you get a plan for the gap: team gap, leader gap, or both, with a clear scope and a price.

What do profile fit and seat fit measure?

Profile fit compares how each leader naturally works with the leadership your five priorities call for. Seat fit compares it with what their current role needs. Both describe fit, not ability or potential, and a partner reads them alongside your own ratings.
