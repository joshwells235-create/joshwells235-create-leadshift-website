How ALIGN works

Six phases, from baseline to proof.

Each workshop phase pairs the session with coaching for the team leader, actions the team commits to, and work to build the habits into how the team runs. Program length: six months.

  1. Phase 1

    Gather team data

    Each leader takes a behavioral assessment, and the team takes The Table Group's Five Dysfunctions of a Team assessment as its baseline.

  2. Phase 2

    Create team awareness

    A workshop on how each leader is wired, what each needs, and how the team works together under pressure, along with a debrief of the team assessment.

  3. Phase 3

    Build trust and manage conflict

    Workshops on trust, the foundation for everything else, and on healthy conflict: debating ideas openly so decisions get better.

  4. Phase 4

    Gain commitment, create accountability

    Workshops on committing to clear decisions and on holding each other to them, so you are not the only one raising the bar.

  5. Phase 5

    Drive team results

    A workshop on putting the team's shared results ahead of individual and department goals.

  6. Phase 6

    Reassess and measure growth

    The team retakes the team assessment, and we compare it with the baseline and the performance measures set at the start.

What is ALIGN?

ALIGN is LeadShift's program for leadership teams. Over six phases, the team works on trust, healthy conflict, commitment, accountability and results, with coaching for the team leader along the way and a reassessment at the end.

Who is ALIGN for?

A CEO or team leader and the leaders who report to them, working on how they operate as one team.

Which assessments does ALIGN use?

Each leader takes a behavioral assessment, and the team takes The Table Group's Five Dysfunctions of a Team assessment at the start and again at the end.

What happens between workshops?

The team leader gets one-on-one coaching, the team works on the commitments it made, and we check progress through pulse checks and debriefs.

How do we know it worked?

The team retakes the team assessment at the end and compares it with the baseline, alongside the performance measures you set with us at the start.
