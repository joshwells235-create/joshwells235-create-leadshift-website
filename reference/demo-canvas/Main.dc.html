Why does every decision still end up on my desk?

Usually because the people around you aren't set up to make it. Leaders without clear ownership send decisions up. A leadership team that avoids hard debate revisits decisions instead of standing behind them. Frontline managers who were never taught to manage pass problems up the line. Each has a different fix, and the Growth Index shows which one you have.

Is the problem my leadership team, or the leaders on it?

Look at where things break. If capable leaders run their own areas well but the team struggles to debate, commit and hold each other accountable, the gap is the team, and ALIGN works on that. If the team works well together but some leaders aren't ready for what the business needs next, the gap is the leaders, and the Leadership Academy develops them in a cohort. Often it's both.

My best performer got promoted and is struggling as a manager. What now?

Being great at the work doesn't teach anyone to run a team. New managers need a simple system for one-on-ones, goals, accountability, recognition and hard conversations, and they need to understand how each person on their team is wired. ASCEND builds both.

Should I develop the leaders I have or replace them?

Start by checking whether they've had a fair shot: clear expectations, the authority to act and real support. When the gap holds after those are in place, a different seat or a change can be the right call for the company and for the leader. The Growth Index puts your ratings beside each leader's match to the role, so the decision rests on more than instinct.

How do we know it's working?

We measure where you start and where you finish. ALIGN gives the team the same team assessment at the beginning and the end, and sets performance measures with the team leader up front. ASCEND scores each manager on the management system before and after.
