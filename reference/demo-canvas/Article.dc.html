How much does leadership development cost?

By [Partner name] · [Date] · [x]-minute read

The short answer

It depends on the program, how many leaders take part, how much one-on-one coaching runs alongside the group work, and how long it runs. At LeadShift, ALIGN for a leadership team runs $50,000 to $75,000 over six months. The Leadership Academy is $15,000 to $18,000 per leader for a nine-month cohort. ASCEND is $4,000 to $6,000 per frontline manager over four months. Executive coaching is $18,000 for six months, and performance and sales coaching are $5,000 per sprint.

What moves the price

Three things move a price inside its range.

  1. How many leaders take part. Seats and managers are priced per person.
  2. How much one-on-one coaching runs alongside the group work. Workshops build a common language; coaching is where each leader applies it.
  3. How long the program runs. A longer program means more sessions and more follow-through.

Pricing will never be fully standardized, and it shouldn't be. That is why we publish ranges rather than one number.

Five questions to ask any firm before you compare prices

  1. How will we measure where we start and where we finish?
  2. How much one-on-one coaching is included, and for whom?
  3. Who delivers it, and will we meet them before we sign?
  4. What happens between sessions?
  5. What is billed separately?
